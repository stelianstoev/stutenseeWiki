# Stutensee Wiki — Glossary

## Article
A discrete piece of practical knowledge about the Reihenhaus (row houses).
Covers both short tips and longer wiki-style entries. May include photos.
House-agnostic — applies to any of the 6 identical houses.
Authored via WYSIWYG editor; stored with version history.

## Category
A hierarchical, community-maintained label for grouping Articles.
An Article may belong to multiple Categories.

## Neighbor
A resident of one of the 6 houses who can view and contribute Articles.
Authenticated via a shared invite code or passphrase.
